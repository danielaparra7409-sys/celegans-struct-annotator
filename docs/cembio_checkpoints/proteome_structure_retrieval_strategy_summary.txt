Interpretation
--------------
The structure retrieval strategy is now defined at the full-proteome level, not only for the 51 NLS candidates.
This makes the workflow reusable for future filters such as secreted proteins, NLS-positive proteins, functional categories, or prioritized candidates.
Since current identifiers are not assumed to be direct UniProt accessions, the initial recommended route is sequence-based search before structural prediction.

Decision
--------
Use proteome_structure_retrieval_strategy.tsv as the general structure retrieval planning table.
The next step is to define the first practical sequence-based search route to reduce structure retrieval time before predicting any new structures.
