-------

Categories were assigned using keyword-based rules applied to combined BLAST, PSI-BLAST, DELTA-BLAST, pSORTB, and AlphaFold2/Foldseek annotations.

Interpretation
--------------
The 51 candidate proteins are not dominated by a single functional group.
The largest initial category is dna_rna_related, followed by toxin/cytotoxic, cell wall/envelope, secretion-system-related, hypothetical/unknown, phage-related, nuclease/endonuclease, transport/membrane, and other functions.
This supports the idea that the NLS candidate subset contains diverse functional signals rather than only generic hypothetical proteins.

Important limitation
--------------------
This is an initial keyword-based classification and must be manually reviewed.
Some proteins may fit more than one biological category, especially proteins with toxin, nuclease, secretion-system, phage, or DNA/RNA-related annotations.
The category column should be treated as a first-pass interpretive layer, not as a final biological assignment.

Decision
--------
Use nls_unique_candidates_functional_categories.tsv as the first categorized version of the curated NLS candidate dataset.
The next step can be manual review of category assignments and preparation of a short summary for the meeting with Jose and/or Maryam.
