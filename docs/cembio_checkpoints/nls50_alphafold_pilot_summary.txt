NLS 50 percent AlphaFold pilot summary
=======================================

Date
----
2026-06-04

Purpose
-------
Retrieve AlphaFold DB structures for the 9 priority SwissProt accessions identified in the
NLS 50% representative pilot search (strong + moderate confidence hits).

This checkpoint closes the AlphaFold retrieval step and documents what was found, what was
not found, and what should be done next.

Context
-------
- 49 NLS cluster representatives at 50% identity were searched against SwissProt.
- 19 of 49 reps matched at least one SwissProt entry (585 total hits).
- 18 unique SwissProt accessions were identified.
- 9 were selected as priority targets (alphafold_lookup_priority <= 2):
    4 strong_sequence_hit
    5 moderate_sequence_hit

Script
------
scripts/fetch_nls50_alphafold_pilot.py
- Queries the AlphaFold EBI API: https://alphafold.ebi.ac.uk/api/prediction/{accession}
- Downloads .cif and .pdb from the versioned URLs returned by the API
- Updates nls50_swissprot_accessions_for_alphafold.tsv in place
- Idempotent: skips rows where alphafold_status != "pending"

Caution
-------
Structures retrieved are for SwissProt homologs, NOT the original CeMbio proteins.
- A SwissProt homolog structure can guide functional interpretation but does NOT
  represent the exact structure of the CeMbio representative protein.
- Sequence identity to SwissProt varies from 32% (Q8X826, Q0IH72) to 84% (P03009).
- Low-pLDDT values (Q8X826=58.78, P15276=61.94) indicate disordered regions; interpret
  those structures with additional caution.
- All functional categories derived from these hits remain exploratory and keyword-based.

Next steps
----------
1. Manual review of 9 downloaded structures (pLDDT profiles, domain coverage).
2. Decide fate of P21679 cluster: ESMFold or ColabFold on CeMbio representative.
3. Review weak/low-confidence hits (priority 3+4, currently "pending") before expanding.
4. After pilot validates, scale retrieval to 36,488 proteome-wide representatives.
